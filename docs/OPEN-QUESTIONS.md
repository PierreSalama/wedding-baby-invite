# Open Questions

These questions do not block keeping this folder as documentation-only, but they should be answered before or during the next rebuild.

## Event details

- What are the final names to display?
- Are the wedding and baby shower on the same date or different dates?
- What are the final venue names and addresses?
- Is there an RSVP deadline?

## Content

- Is the homepage headline more wedding-led, baby-led, or equally combined?
- Do you want a short story section or mostly logistics?
- Do you have real photos ready, or should the next build use premium placeholders first?
- Do you want a countdown section, or is that optional?

## RSVP behavior

- Should guests be able to respond for multiple people in one household?
- Is phone number required, or only optional?
- Do you want email collected at all?
- Do you want a food preference field later, or not in v1?

## Access model

- Is one public shared link still acceptable?
- Do you want a private invite code eventually, or definitely not?

## Design

- Which green direction is right: sage, eucalyptus, olive, or deeper botanical green?
- Do you want any small gold accent at all, or pure green-and-white only?
- Do you want the homepage to feel more floral, more modern editorial, or more classic invitation?

## Operational

- Is a free `pages.dev` domain enough at first?
- Do you already have a preferred custom domain name?
- Who will log into the admin dashboard and manage RSVPs?

## Technical

- Do you still want Cloudflare D1 plus Pages, or do you want the next chat to re-evaluate the stack?
- Should the next build keep a dedicated `/admin`, or would CSV-only storage be enough if that cuts complexity?
